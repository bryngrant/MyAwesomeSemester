## About this course

The purpose of this class is to improve Development, concepts, and impact of sports media, including roles and responsibilities of
journalists, production staff, and public relations professionals. I am taking this class as an elective but I was also intrigued because it is something out of my comfort zone. I am hoping to get a better understanding of sports and sports media from this class.  

## About Bill Roth

Bill Roth graduated from Syracuse University’s S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications with a degree in broadcast journalism. At Virginia Tech he teaches many courses in Sports Media and Analytics. He is also an active announcer for Virginia Tech known as “Voice of the Hokies.”

### Research interests

He does not have any research because he is a professor of practice. However, he focuses on sports media, broadcasting, journalism, and sports analytics. His work explores how sports stories are reported and presented through television, radio, and other forms of media.

### Publications

He does not have any publications but has his own YouTube channel and broadcasting reports.
-"The Roth Report"
- Hosts "Virginia Tech Athletics podcasts"

### Service and outreach

Bill has helped build partnerships with local radio stations and other sports media outlets so students can gain experience outside the classroom. He also helps mentor students who express interest going into Sports Media field.
