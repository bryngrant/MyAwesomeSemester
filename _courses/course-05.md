## About Carlos Evia

Professor Evia got his Masters Degree in Computer Systems at Universidad La Salle in Mexico City as well as his Ph.D. in Technical Communication and Rhetoric from Texas Tech University. He teaches courses about content management, technology and professional communication. He also uses AI to show how technology is changing the way people communicate. 

### Research interests

He studies technical communication, digital content management, automation, and artificial intelligence. This research involves finding better ways for organizations to create, organize, reuse, and share digital information.

### Publications

-"Outsourcing Technical Communication: Issues, Policies, and Practices"

### Service and outreach

Professor Evia is well versed in service and outreach. He leads the Tech for Humanity initiative, he represents his college on university technology committees, he also is involved with El Centro at Virginia Tech.
