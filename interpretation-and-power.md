  <p class="article-meta">Encoding/decoding and the politics of meaning control</p>

  <p>
    Stuart Hall's framework lets you show who gets to shape the “preferred meaning” of a message and who must negotiate or resist that meaning.
    This is especially relevant for algorithmic platforms where distribution rules influence what audiences can even see.
  </p>
  <p>
    In your final content, identify a communication problem and map dominant, negotiated, and oppositional readings.
    Highlight how platform design and institutional authority interact.
